Before you go

  • sign the contract and get the £2k sorted before Day 1 starts
  • Day 1 has to be Mon–Sat, Preston Market Hall is shut Sundays (open 8am–5pm)
  • Day 3 on a Saturday if poss, streets are busiest
  • Day 2 night activity depends on what day it is, see Day 2 evening
  • xmas markets are on till 22 Dec, makes Day 2 and 3 way easier cos people are chilled and browsing
  • gets dark about 4pm so all the street stuff is daytime
