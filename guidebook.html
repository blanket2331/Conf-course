How it works

Like the gym. Pick exercises at the right level, do the reps, rest, move up when it gets easy. He doesn't have to do every exercise – pick depending on how he's feeling that day.

Before each exercise ask him "how nervous, 0–10?"

0–3too easy – move up a level or do the harder version
4–7right level – stay here
8–10too much – drop down or easier version, warm up again

Ask again every 3–5 reps. It usually drops 2–3 points after a set, that's when you move him up.

Levels

LevelWhat
0 Prepdrills with you, no strangers
1 Presenceeye contact, saying hi, how he carries himself
2 Everyday chatsquick normal stuff, nothing romantic
3 Comfort zone breakersembarrassing on purpose, getting nos
4 Convos2 min+, actual back and forth

Each session

  1. Warm up (10–15 mins) – a level 0 drill, then a set one level below today's
  2. Main sets – 3 sets of 45–60 mins, debrief after each
  3. Finisher – one rep a bit above his level, only if he's feeling good
  4. Cool down – final debrief, count up, homework

What to tell him

Go through this with him on the morning of Day 1, before he signs.

Why he gets nervous

  • his body treats it like a threat, same as public speaking – heart racing, mind going blank
  • it's learned so it can be unlearned. only way is doing it loads in small steps till his body stops reacting
  • he can already talk to guys so his social skills are fine, this is just getting his body to calm down

What to expect

  • first 3–5 of each day are the hardest. nerves peak right before he speaks then drop off fast once he's going
  • after 10–15 at one level it gets boring – that's what we want
  • there'll be awkward ones and nos, they all count

The rules

  • when we tell you to do something you do it – that's the contract
  • 3 second rule – when we say go you're moving by "1", no circling
  • we care that you did it, not how she reacted
  • be honest with your 0–10, it's how we pick what you do next
  • follow the rules every time

Keep in mind

  • most girls are flattered or don't care either way, it's not a big deal to them
  • a no is about her day not you – she's busy, taken, tired
  • no one's watching, everyone's in their own head
  • walking off nicely is a win
  • every rep counts, even the bad ones

Rules

Every exercise, whatever we tell him.

Don't approach

  • anyone who looks under 18 – if in doubt, don't
  • headphones in, on the phone, rushing, or with kids
  • anyone who can't easily walk off – lifts, bus stops, stuck in a queue, cash machines
  • staff for anything romantic, they're at work (fine for everyday chats)

Always

  • come from the front or side so she sees you, never from behind
  • arm's length away, slightly side on
  • first no is final – "no worries, have a good day" and go
  • no touching, nothing about her body, no following, never the same girl twice
  • don't film or take pics of anyone without asking

Reading her

Keep going

  • turns towards him, stops walking
  • longer answers, adds her own stuff
  • asks him things back
  • proper smile, laughing
  • takes an earphone out, puts bags down

Wrap it up

  • feet still pointing away, edging off
  • one word answers
  • doesn't ask anything
  • tight polite smile, looking round or at her phone
  • holding bags tighter, arms crossed

One on its own might just be nerves. 2+ wrap-up signs = he finishes nicely within 10 secs.

What to bring

Coaches

  • signed contract
  • tally – notes app or a clicker
  • stopwatch on phone for day 2 convos
  • this guidebook

Him

  • a few openers saved on his phone – only looks between sets
  • charged phone + power bank, he needs it for numbers
  • £30–50 for coffees, lunch, salsa etc
  • mints + water, nerves = dry mouth
  • decent fit – clothes that fit, clean shoes, hair sorted. and a warm coat
L0

Prep

With you, no strangers. Use as the warm up and any time he's 8+.

0.1Posture reset – look and feel more confident in 10 secs
How
feet shoulder width, roll shoulders up, back, down, chin level, hands loose. hold 10 secs
Reps
3 at the start, then 1 before every approach
Watch for
shoulders creeping forward as the day goes on – just say "shoulders"
0.2Calming breath – bring his heart rate down fast
How
2 quick breaths in through the nose (1 full + a top up), 1 long slow breath out through the mouth
Reps
2–3 whenever he's 7+
Watch for
fast shallow breathing, fidgeting, rubbing hands = time for this
0.3Eye contact hold – get used to holding eye contact
How
face each other, hold eye contact, no talking
Reps
3 × 30 secs
Watch for
laughing, looking away, staring too hard – should be relaxed not intense
Harder
hold it while you ask him questions
0.4Voice – get heard first time
How
stand 5m apart on a busy street, he says openers so you hear them clearly. ends flat or going down, not like a question
Say e.g
"excuse me, quick question" / "hi, I'm ___" / "had to say hello"
Reps
5 lines × 2
Watch for
mumbling, rushing, going up at the end – repeat till it's right
0.5Role play – 4 replies – nothing catches him off guard
How
he opens on you, you play 4 girls:
  • warm – chatty, asks stuff back → he keeps it going
  • busy – "sorry I'm in a rush" → "no worries, have a good day", leaves
  • taken – "I've got a boyfriend" → "lucky guy, nice talking to you"
  • cold – one word answers, looking away → spots it, wraps up in 10 secs
Reps
each once, then 2 more rounds mixed up
Watch for
arguing, trying to convince her, looking gutted – exit should be quick and light
0.6Threading ping pong – never run out of stuff to say
How
you say a random sentence, he picks one word from it and builds on it with a statement, not a question
Say e.g
you: "just got back from Leeds" → him: "Leeds, you look like a city break type"
Reps
10 in a row, no gaps over 3 secs
Harder
you only give one word answers, he keeps it going 60 secs
L1

Presence

No proper talking yet. Good for a nervous start or resetting after a few bad ones.

1.1Slow walk – take up space
How
walk the length of a busy street (Fishergate, Market St) at about 70% his normal speed, good posture, looking ahead not at the floor
Reps
2 lengths
Watch for
speeding up, head down, hands in pockets
1.2Eye contact + smile – quick connection with strangers
How
girls walking towards him – relaxed eye contact 1–2 secs, small smile or nod, then look away naturally. no staring
Reps
20
Watch for
looking away the second she looks back, forced smile. count how many smile back – usually most, good for his confidence
1.3Hello walk – use his voice with zero pressure
How
says hi as he walks past people and keeps walking
Say e.g
"hiya" / "morning"
Reps
20, at least 10 girls
Watch for
saying it so quiet no one hears – only counts if they react
Harder
add a compliment e.g "hiya, nice coat"
L2

Everyday chats

Most of Day 1. Nothing romantic.

2.1Time / directions – start a chat for an obvious reason
How
stop a girl walking at a normal pace, ask the time or directions, wait for her full answer, thank her, smile, go
Say e.g
"excuse me, do you know the time?" / "which way's the train station?" / "is there a Caffè Nero round here?"
Reps
10
Watch for
saying sorry loads, leaving before she's finished, looking at the floor
Harder
add one line after e.g "cheers, are you from round here?"
2.2Recommendation – get her opinion, slightly longer chat
How
in a shop, ask a girl working there or shopping which of 2 things she'd pick
Say e.g
"which one would you get for your mum?" / "is this aftershave nice or a bit much?"
Reps
5–10
Watch for
not looking at her while she answers
Harder
follow up e.g "why that one?"
2.3Compliment + keep walking – say something nice, expect nothing back
How
quick genuine compliment on something she chose (style, coat, bag, shoes), keeps walking, doesn't wait for a reply
Say e.g
"love that coat" / "your style's sick"
Reps
5
Watch for
anything about her body – stick to stuff she chose
2.4Queue / café comment – chat off something you're both doing
How
in a queue, light comment to the girl next to him
Say e.g
"is it always this busy?" / "have you had anything here before, what's good?"
Reps
5
Watch for
giving up after one line – aim for 2–3 back and forths
2.5Order + one – one extra line when he buys something
How
whenever he orders anything, adds one line to whoever's serving
Say e.g
"what's your fave thing on here?" / "long shift today?"
Reps
every time he buys something, all 3 days
L3

Comfort zone breakers

Embarrassing on purpose and getting nos, so Day 3 feels easy. Pick 3–5 a session, each one = 1 rep.

3.1Ask for a discount – hear a no and realise it's nothing
How
market stall or independent shop, ask for money off something cheap with a smile
Say e.g
"any chance you'd do it for a quid?" / "is there a student discount? I'm not a student"
Watch for
apologising or bottling it
3.2Photo request – ask a favour, then ask for more
How
ask a girl to take his pic at a landmark (Wallace & Gromit statue outside Preston Market Hall, the big wheel at the xmas markets), then ask for one more
Say e.g
"one more, a serious one this time"
Watch for
not doing the second ask
3.3Settle a debate – open with something playful
How
stop a girl or a group to settle a made up argument between him and you
Say e.g
"settle a debate, is a hotdog a sandwich?" / "pineapple on pizza, yes or no?" / "who's better looking, me or him?"
Watch for
leaving straight after her answer – react and add a line
3.4Rate my fit – get judged and be fine with it
How
ask a girl to rate his outfit out of 10
Say e.g
"be honest, out of 10 would you let your mate go out dressed like this?"
Watch for
getting defensive at a low score – laugh it off e.g "harsh but fair"
3.5Public embarrassment – being looked at is harmless
How
pick one:
  • lie on a bench for 60 secs
  • walk a whole street holding one banana
  • ask a busker for a song request
  • 10 star jumps in a square
Watch for
rushing it – he should notice no one cares
3.6Rejection hunt – collect 5 nos
How
odd requests where a no is likely. only nos count
Say e.g
"can I have a free sample?" (where there isn't any) / "swap your coffee for my water?" / "can I stand behind the counter for a pic?"
Watch for
picking easy ones that get yeses
When
best one for the morning of Day 3 if he's dreading rejection
L4

Convos

2 min+, time it from his first line. Make sure he knows reading her first.

4.1Opinion opener – easy question that turns into a convo
How
stop a girl with a question about a choice, react to her answer and keep going
Say e.g
"quick one, need a girl's opinion – getting my sister a present, candle or scarf?" / "me and my mate can't agree where to eat, where would you go round here?"
Reps
3–5
Watch for
thanking her and leaving after the answer – that's just a level 2
4.2Situational opener – comment on what's in front of you
How
comment on what she's carrying, doing, or the place
Say e.g
"that's a lot of shopping, good day?" / "what's actually worth getting at these stalls, I'm overwhelmed" / "you look like you know your vintage, anything in here worth it?"
Reps
3–5
Watch for
anything that sounds like a dig – keep it playful
4.3Statements only – stop it feeling like an interview
How
after the opener, no questions for 60 secs. keeps it going with statements, observations, stuff about himself
Say e.g
"you look like you're on a mission" / "I'm terrible at presents, always end up getting socks"
Reps
3
Watch for
sneaking questions in e.g "…right?"
4.430 sec stretch – push past where he'd normally leave
How
when he wants to leave he stays 30 secs longer – only if she's still showing interest
Reps
every convo
Watch for
staying when she's giving wrap-up signs
4.5Cold reads – make it personal and playful
How
friendly guess about her. if he's wrong she corrects him and it keeps going anyway
Say e.g
"you look like the organised one in your friend group" / "I reckon you're a dog person" / "you're not from round here are you?"
Reps
1+ every convo
Watch for
guesses about her looks, age or background
4.6Names – make it a proper intro
How
about a minute in, introduce himself, get her name, use it once later on
Say e.g
"I'm ___ by the way" … "so Sarah, what do you actually do when you're not shopping?"
Reps
every convo
4.7Group intro – join a group with girls in it
How
at the evening thing, walk up to a table or group, introduce himself and you, join in
Say e.g
"hey, we're new here, mind if we join?" / "what you playing? room for 2 more?"
Reps
2 a night
Watch for
only talking to the girl he likes – include everyone, get on with the lads too
L5

The ask

Do 0.5 and the role play in 5.2 before the first real one.

5.1Direct opener – just say why he stopped her
How
from the front, stop at arm's length, say why he stopped her, then straight into a statement or question so she's not stuck replying to a compliment
Say e.g
"hey, saw you walking past and had to say hello, I'm ___" → "you look like you're on a mission, where you off to?" / "this is random but you look great and I'd kick myself if I didn't say hi"
Reps
3–5
Watch for
long build up before getting to the point, nervous laugh after
Easier
use a level 4 opener and save it for the ask
5.2The number ask – ask straight, at the right time
When
2–5 mins in, she's showing 2+ interest signs, convo's still going well (not dying)
How
  1. reason to go – "I've got to meet my mate, but…"
  2. why – "…I've enjoyed talking to you"
  3. the ask – "let's get a coffee sometime, what's your number?"
  4. hand her his phone on a new contact, shut up and wait
  5. if she hesitates – "or I can give you mine?"
Role play
with you first, 5 times: 3 yes, 1 no, 1 hesitating
Reps
5 (the Day 3 target)
Watch for
hedging e.g "I don't suppose you'd maybe…", going up at the end like a question, filling the silence, not holding the phone out

no"no worries, have a good day" and goes. no convincing

5.4Solo – do it without you there
How
you wait in a café or out of sight, he does a full approach + ask on his own, then comes back and tells you what happened
Reps
1–2, only after 3+ asks with you watching
5.5Coffee now – advanced, quick date there and then
How
if it's going really well and she's not in a rush, invite her for a quick coffee instead of asking for the number
Say e.g
"I'm about to grab a coffee, come for 20 mins?"
Watch for
pushing if she's busy – just go to the number
5.6Text after – turn the number into a meet up
How
text the same day so she knows who it is, suggest something specific in the next 2–3 days. no double texting if she doesn't reply
Say e.g
"hey Sarah, it's ___ from King St, the one with terrible gift ideas 😄 coffee Thursday?"

On the day

Before each approach

"shoulders" → "breathe" → "her, green coat" → "3, 2, 1, go"

Drop the first 2 once he's warmed up. By Day 3 he picks who.

Debrief every 3–5 reps

2 mins, away from her.

  1. what did you do well? – he answers first, has to be specific
  2. what was her body language saying?
  3. one thing to change – just one
  4. how nervous now, 0–10? – use it to pick the next exercise

Coaching

  • praise what he did not how it went e.g "good volume" not "unlucky"
  • do the first couple yourself, fastest way to calm him down
  • stand 10–20m back – close enough to see, far enough you're not part of it
  • never laugh at a rep, never give feedback where she can hear
